One short message. Two minutes. Add your voice to the count.

Any American can write to the President through the official White House contact form. Below is a short, respectful, non-partisan message that people across party lines can put their name to. It deliberately says what we want, not what we're against — a list of complaints could run for pages; a few shared goals fit in a paragraph. Copy it, change it to your own words if you like, send it, and tell us you did.

What this count is, and isn't

  
    
    
  • It is the number of times someone has clicked "I sent it" on this site, limited to once per person per day.
    • 
    
  • It is self-reported. Nobody outside the White House can see what is submitted through its form, so we cannot verify sends, and we don't claim to.
    • 
    
  • It includes everyone who sent a version of the message — edited in their own words or sent before a wording change. More on how it works, and what people ask →
    • 
  


  
What we store

  
    
    
  • The count. One number.
    • 
    
  • Nothing about you. No name, email, address, cookies, accounts, or analytics. The form you fill in belongs to the White House; this site never sees it. We keep a few running totals — page views and button clicks, as plain numbers, nothing per visitor — so we can see what share of visitors go on to send.
    • 
    
  • For abuse prevention only: when you click "I sent it", the server keeps a one-way, salted hash of your internet address for 24 hours so the same connection isn't counted twice in a day. The address itself is never stored, the hash is deleted after a day, and the salt changes daily so hashes can't be linked across days. A Cloudflare "human check" runs on the click to keep bots out.
    • 
    
  • Server access logging is turned off. The complete source code is public, so anyone can check all of the above: Source code on GitHub.
